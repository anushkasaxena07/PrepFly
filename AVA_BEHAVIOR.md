# PrepFly Ava: Human-Like AI Interviewer Specification & Behavior Model

Ava is PrepFly's track-aware, resume-grounded, personality-driven conversational AI interviewer.
She conducts 1-on-1 technical and behavioral job interviews that behave like natural human conversations rather than robotic question-list bots.

---

## 1. System Architecture & Conversation Model

Ava operates on a strict **LLM vs. Deterministic Code split (Operating Rule R1)**:
- **LLM Responsibility**: Spoken dialogue generation (warm greetings, active-listening follow-ups, conceptual probes, natural closings) and intent classification.
- **Deterministic Code Responsibility**: ALL state machine transitions, silence timers, question budgets, difficulty adaptation, track scope enforcement, and scoring rubrics.

```
Candidate Audio / Text ──> [Intent Classifier (16 Intents)]
                                   │
                                   ├──> [Candidate Control Handler] (Repeat, Clarify, Hint, Pause, Early Exit)
                                   └──> [Interview State Machine] (Greeting -> Briefing -> Questioning -> Closing)
                                               │
                                               ▼
                                  [Track Strategy Controller (8 Tracks)]
                                  + [Resume Grounding Fact Sheet]
                                               │
                                               ▼
                                  [Deterministic Scope Guard (Rule R2)]
                                               │
                                               ▼
                                 [Personality Dialogue Engine (5 Profiles)]
                                               │
                                               ▼
                               [Real-time Synthesizer / Audio Cutoff Manager]
```

---

## 2. Conversation Lifecycle & State Transitions

Ava enforces a deterministic multi-stage interview progression:

1. `PRE_START`: Session initialized, audio/video devices verified, DPDP affirmative consent confirmed.
2. `GREETING`: Ava introduces herself warmly, establishes rapport, and welcomes the candidate by name.
3. `ICEBREAKER`: Light, non-evaluative check-in (e.g. "How is your day going so far?"). Responses are NEVER scored.
4. `FORMAT_BRIEFING`: Ava explains the interview track, expected duration, structure, and candidate controls (asking for repeats, hints, clarifications, or pauses).
5. `MAIN_QUESTIONING`:
   - Substate `ASKED`: Question presented with subtitle captions and spoken audio.
   - Substate `LISTENING`: Candidate speech recognition active with live audio level meter.
   - Substate `PROBING`: Active-listening follow-up probe quoting the candidate's exact words.
   - Substate `TRANSITION`: Varied bridging statements avoiding consecutive repetition.
6. `WRAP_UP_QUESTIONS`: Candidate is given an opportunity to ask questions about the role or company.
7. `CLOSING`: Ava thanks the candidate for their time and transitions to the evaluation phase.
8. `ENDED`: Session concluded; instant audio cutoff triggered; final report compiled.

## 5. Candidate Control & Intent Detection

Ava recognizes 16+ conversational intents in real time. Non-answer turns are **NEVER scored as zero or poor answers**:

- **Early Exit (`end_interview_request`)**: Cleanly terminates the session with `audio_cutoff: true`. The candidate receives a provisional evaluation report instead of a punitive "Strong Reject".
- **Repeat Request (`repeat_question`)**: Ava repeats or rephrases the question without penalty.
- **Clarification Request (`clarification_request`)**: Ava provides requirement specifications without penalty.
- **Hint Request (`hint_request`)**: Ava provides a small conceptual hint based on the personality's hint policy.
- **Pause / Resume (`pause_request`, `resume_request`)**: Pauses the session and silence timer until the candidate indicates they are ready.
- **Small Talk / Rapport (`small_talk`)**: Friendly human-like pleasantries that do not contaminate the technical scorecard.

---

## 6. Silence Management & Barge-In Ducking

- **Silence Escalation Ladder**:
  - `Level 0 (0-14s)`: Normal candidate thinking pause.
  - `Level 1 (Check-in)`: Gentle inquiry ("Do you need the question repeated?").
  - `Level 2 (Offer Help)`: Small collaborative nudge or hint.
  - `Level 3 (Offer Skip)`: Option to advance to the next question.
  - `Level 4 (Auto-Pause)`: Safe pause preserving candidate session state.
- **Barge-in Ducking**: If candidate begins speaking while Ava is speaking, speech synthesis and audio playback are immediately ducked and cancelled, seamlessly transitioning Ava into `LISTENING` substate.

---

## 7. Zero Audio Bleed / Instant Audio Cutoff Invariant

When an interview ends (early exit or completion), Ava's voice **instantly terminates**:
1. `window.speechSynthesis.cancel()` is immediately invoked on the client.
2. `currentAudioRef.current.pause()` halts any in-flight HTML5 Audio element.
3. `speechQueue.current` is cleared.
4. `isEndingRef.current = true` prevents any in-flight or delayed `/next` promises from starting speech synthesis.
5. In-flight and background threads terminate gracefully.

---

## 8. Report Structure with Turn-Referenced Conversation Summary

Every generated report contains a structured `conversation_summary` referencing individual turns (`turn_1`, `turn_2`, ...):
- Segregates substantive technical answers from non-scoring conversational turns.
- Preserves verbatim candidate quotes as evidence.
- Generates a chronological multi-turn narrative of the interview progression.
- Retains objective personality neutrality: identical candidate answers across all 5 personalities yield identical rubric scores.
